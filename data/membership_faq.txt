Falcons Club Membership FAQ

How do I join?
Complete the membership form at the Northside Community Sports Hall front desk and attend a short welcome session. The club welcomes new members throughout the year.

How much does membership cost?
The standard fee is $25 per month. Students aged 12–17 pay $15 per month. The first welcome session is free. Fees are reviewed each January; ask the club desk to confirm the current rate before joining.

What should I bring to my first session?
Wear comfortable sports clothing and indoor trainers. Bring a refillable water bottle. The club can lend basic equipment for your first two visits.

Where does the club meet?
Regular sessions are held at Northside Community Sports Hall, 18 Meadow Lane. Check the monthly event schedule for the room and session time.

Can I bring a friend?
Yes. Guests may attend the Saturday community scrimmage if they check in at reception and stay with their member host. Contact the club desk before bringing a guest to another session.

How do I contact the club?
Email hello@falconsclub.example or speak with staff at the sports hall front desk.
