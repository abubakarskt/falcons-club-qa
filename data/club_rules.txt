Falcons Club Rules
Last updated: September 2026

1. Be respectful. Members must treat teammates, visitors, coaches, and facility staff with courtesy. Harassment, discrimination, and deliberate intimidation are not acceptable.
2. Use equipment safely. Follow coach instructions, warm up before training, and return shared equipment to its labeled storage area after use. Report damaged equipment to a coach.
3. Keep the space tidy. Take personal belongings and rubbish with you. Food is allowed only in the lobby; water bottles with secure lids may be taken into the gym.
4. Attend responsibly. If you cannot attend a session you signed up for, notify the club at least 12 hours beforehand whenever possible. Arrive 10 minutes early for scheduled events.
5. Guests must check in with the front desk and remain with their host member. Members are responsible for making sure their guests follow club rules.
6. Repeated or serious rule violations may result in a conversation with the club committee and temporary suspension of membership.
